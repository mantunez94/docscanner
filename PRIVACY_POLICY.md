# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** May 26, 2026

## Summary

DocScanner is a **100% offline** document scanner. **No data ever leaves your device.** There are no servers, no analytics, no ads, and no user accounts.

---

## Data Collection

**DocScanner does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.**

The app has no internet permission, no network calls, and no third-party analytics SDKs. It functions entirely offline.

## Permissions

| Permission | Purpose |
|------------|---------|
| **Camera** | To capture document images for scanning. No images are sent anywhere. |
| **Storage** | To save scanned PDFs and JPEGs to your device's local storage and gallery. |

Permissions are requested at runtime only when the relevant feature is used.

## Third-Party Services

DocScanner uses the following third-party libraries, all of which operate **entirely on-device** with no network access:

- **Google ML Kit Text Recognition** (`google_mlkit_text_recognition`) — OCR runs locally on your device. No text or image data is sent to Google.
- **OpenCV** (`opencv_dart`) — Image processing (edge detection, perspective correction, enhancement). Fully offline.
- **Gal** (`gal`) — Saves images to the device gallery. No network access.

## Data Storage

- All scanned documents, PDFs, and images are stored **locally** on your device.
- App preferences (theme, onboarding completion) are stored locally via `SharedPreferences`.
- You can delete all app data at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing app storage from system settings.

## Children's Privacy

DocScanner does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13. The app has no data collection mechanisms at all.

## Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date at the top will be revised. Since the app is fully offline and has no communication channel, changes will be reflected in the app's store listing.
